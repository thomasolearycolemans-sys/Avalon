HOW THE NIGHT WORKS

Everyone closes their eyes and makes a fist. The narrator then calls each group in a fixed order: the Cleric's check first, then Evil's council opens its eyes, then thumbs go up for Merlin, then the special callings — the Untrustworthy Servant, Percival, the Lancelots, the Messengers — each opening and closing in turn, until dawn is counted in. Who is called, and who is excluded, depends entirely on which roles you tick at setup.
